61 firsts, and who made them

The list starts with Earliest documented military rockets (Jin 'fire arrows', siege of Kaifeng) (1232) and runs to Starship Flight 14 — first orbital Starship flight (26 Starlink V3) (2026-09-28). The USA made 23 of these firsts, the USSR and Russia 18, China 11, and Europe, Japan, India and international projects the other 9. Each entry gives the date, who did it, the vehicle and why it matters.
