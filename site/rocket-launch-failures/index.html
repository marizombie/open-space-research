WHAT WENT WRONG

Rocket and spacecraft failures since 1957: what went wrong and whether the fix held

45 rocket and spacecraft failures from 1957 to 2026, with the root cause, the lesson learned, the fix and whether it held on later flights. Plus every orbital launch failure of 2025 and 2026.

What the 45 failures show

This catalogue holds 45 failures of rockets and spacecraft from 1957 to 2026: well-known accidents, plus every orbital launch failure of 2026. Each entry gives what happened, the lesson, the fix, and whether the fix held on later flights, with the source.

  • Engines and propellant systems caused the most failures: 16 of 45. 7 failures, all from 2026, have no public cause.
  • Did the fix work? It held for 18 failures and did not for 6. 8 vehicles have not flown again yet.
  • The same kinds of failure keep coming back. In 30 of the 40 failures we can check, the same kind of failure also hit another flight.
  • 5 failures killed 96 people: Nedelin catastrophe (1960, 78), Apollo 1 fire (1967, 3), Soyuz 1 (1967, 1), Challenger STS-51-L (1986, 7), Columbia STS-107 (2003, 7).
